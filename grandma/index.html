For Grandma Patty

Help us build the family tree

Hi Momma,

Pixie and I have started something for the boys, and we would love your help with it. We are gathering the story of our family, all of it: who we come from, where everyone was born, how everyone met, the faces and the places. One day Hudson and Hendrix will have the whole thing in their hands, a family tree with real pictures and real voices, and you are a big part of it.

This page is the first step. Fill in whatever you know, skip whatever you don't, and come back as often as you like. It remembers your answers on this device. Near the bottom are some bigger questions we would love to sit down and talk through with you at Christmas, and a way to send us pictures.

Fifty years of teaching, and now you get to be the one answering the questions. No grades, I promise.

Love you,
Christopher

For when we are together

No need to write these down. Read them ahead so they can simmer, and tap the ones you would love to talk about. We will bring a recorder so the boys can hear your voice telling it.

  • What is your earliest memory?
  • What was your house like growing up? Walk us through it, room by room.
  • What were your parents like? What did you learn from each of them?
  • What did you want to be when you grew up?
  • How did you become a teacher? Is there one student you still think about?
  • How did you and Pop Pop meet?
  • What was Christopher like as a boy? What did he get in trouble for?
  • What did raising a son teach you that nobody warned you about?
  • What is the hardest thing you have lived through, and what got you through it?
  • What are you most proud of?
  • What do you know now that you wish you had known at 20?
  • Is there a family story everybody retells?
  • What do you hope Hudson and Hendrix carry from our family?
