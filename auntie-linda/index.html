For Auntie Linda

Help us build the family tree

Hi Auntie Linda,

Pixie and I have started something for the boys, and we would love your help with it. We are gathering the story of our family, all of it: who we come from, where everyone was born, how everyone met, the faces and the places. One day Hudson and Hendrix will have the whole thing in their hands, a family tree with real pictures and real voices.

You grew up right alongside Mom, so you hold a piece of this nobody else can: the Rumsey side, and what the two of you were like as girls. Mom has her own page with the same questions about your parents and grandparents, and two memories of the same people are better than one.

Fill in whatever you know, skip whatever you don’t, and come back as often as you like. It remembers your answers on this device. Near the bottom are some bigger questions we would love to talk through with you when we are together over the holidays, and a way to send us pictures.

With love,
Christopher

For when we are together

No need to write these down. Read them ahead so they can simmer, and tap the ones you would love to talk about. We will bring a recorder so the boys can hear your voice telling it.

  • What is your earliest memory?
  • What was your house like growing up? Walk us through it, room by room.
  • What were your parents like? What did you learn from each of them?
  • What did you want to be when you grew up?
