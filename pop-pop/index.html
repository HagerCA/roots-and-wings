Hi Dad,

Pixie and I have started something for the boys, and we would love your help with it. We are gathering the story of our family, all of it: who we come from, where everyone was born, how everyone met, the faces and the places. One day Hudson and Hendrix will have the whole thing in their hands, a family tree with real pictures and real voices.

You are the photographer of this family, so you are already holding a big piece of it: your mom and dad, Marty and Betty, through your lens. There is a section just for them, a section about you behind the camera, and a space for you and Zozo, because that part of the story is yours to tell.

Fill in whatever you know, skip whatever you don't, and come back as often as you like. It remembers your answers on this device. Near the bottom are some bigger questions we would love to talk through with you in person, and a way to send us pictures.

Love you,
Christopher

For when we are together

No need to write these down. Read them ahead so they can simmer, and tap the ones you would love to talk about. We will bring a recorder so the boys can hear your voice telling it.

  • What were your parents like? What did you learn from each of them?

  • What is the one photograph you wish you had taken?
  • How did you and Grandma Patty meet?
  • How did you and Zozo meet?
  • What was Christopher like as a boy?
  • What did your working life look like, from your first job on?
